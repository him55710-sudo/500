# Independent visual review

Reviewed the supplied reference photographs against final CAM_A/B/C and desk,
lamp and plant detail screenshots. The final review found no large visible
regression blocking delivery of the music-salon visual slice. This is not a
certification of AAA quality or the full game.

Confirmed improvements: books contact the supporting shelf and have varied
leather spines, wear and thickness. Superseded gold bands and repeated black
shadows are gone. The vessel, lamp base and white succulent pot contact the desk.
Candles have visible flames without black cards. The envelope fold and pressed
seal perimeter are visible. Original story figurines and room layout remain.

The reviewer identified a gap between the lamp pull chain and its handle. Source
geometry confirmed a 0.73 cm separation. A 0.95 cm brass connector now overlaps
both ends in the saved scene; regenerated source includes the same connector.

Remaining reference gaps: the rug's silhouette remains thin and its pile reads
more like a printed surface at close range. Bright plant leaves lose some color
and relief under the window light. The desk and walls have less localized wear
than the references. Original figurine joints and regular furniture geometry
retain a stylized construction, and some large wall areas remain sparse.

Further art passes would focus on rug pile, localized contact wear, and selected
original furniture joinery. These are disclosed art limitations beyond the
bounded flagship pass, not hidden by post-processing or an arbitrary score.
